# Free Learning Resources — FDE + AI Engineering

> **100% free YouTube channels + websites** for the one-language (JS/TS) Fullstack + AI path.
> No paid courses needed. Pick **one** per topic, finish it, then move on.

**Beginner rule:** Website first (read/skim), then a YouTube video on the same topic, then build a tiny thing. Repeat.
